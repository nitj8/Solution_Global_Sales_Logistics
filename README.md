# Solution_Global_Sales_Logistics
1. Project Title / Headline

🚚 WallMort Retailers Logistics Performance Dashboard | Power BI Report
A comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor and optimize logistics operations—focusing on shipping trends, delivery performance, and cost efficiency across different segments and product categories.

2. Short Description / Purpose

The Logistics Overview Dashboard provides real-time visibility into key supply chain metrics such as total orders, shipping time, and delivery costs. It empowers logistics teams and managers to evaluate operational efficiency, identify delivery bottlenecks, and ensure on-time shipment performance for better customer satisfaction.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:
• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Used for data modeling, dashboard design, and visualization.
• 🔄 Power Query Editor – For data extraction, transformation, and cleaning.
• 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – For calculating KPIs like Average Shipping Days, SLA Performance, and Cost Metrics.
• 🗂️ Data Modeling – Established relationships among Order, Segment, and Shipping tables for seamless cross-filtering.
• 💾 File Format – .pbix (development) and .png (presentation preview).

4. Data Source

Source: WallMort Retailers’ logistics and order fulfillment dataset (sample retail operations data).
The dataset includes:

Order Details (Order Date, Ship Mode, Segment, Product Name)

Shipping and Fulfillment Times

SLA (Service Level Agreement) Compliance Data

Shipping Costs and Delivery Performance

The data was cleaned and structured in Power Query, aggregated monthly and by ship mode for trend analysis.

5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Retail companies like WallMort face challenges in tracking logistics performance across thousands of orders. Identifying delays, cost fluctuations, and SLA compliance issues manually can lead to inefficiencies, missed SLAs, and customer dissatisfaction.

• Goal of the Dashboard

To develop an interactive logistics performance tracker that allows users to:

Monitor total orders, shipping days, and average shipping costs.

Identify trends in delivery times and costs over time.

Measure SLA compliance (on-time vs. delayed orders).

Analyze shipping mode performance for operational improvement.

Enable data-driven logistics decisions with visual insights.

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Top KPIs (Top Row):

Total Orders: 51,290

Average Shipping Days: 3.97 days

Average Shipping Cost: $26.376
These summarize logistics performance at a glance.

Shipping Trend Over Time (Line + Area Chart):
Shows monthly trends for both Fulfillment Days and Shipping Costs, helping to spot peak periods or inefficiencies.

Total Orders by SLA Status (Donut Chart):
Displays the share of On-time, Delayed, and Within SLA orders, indicating delivery performance compliance (68.18% within SLA).

Total Orders by Ship Mode (Bar Chart):
Compares different shipping methods — Standard, Second Class, First Class, and Same Day — helping to assess cost vs. efficiency trade-offs.

Filter Panel (Right Side):
Interactive filters for Order Date, Segment, and Product Name allow users to drill down into specific timeframes or customer groups.

• Business Impact & Insights

Operational Efficiency: Provided clear visibility into delivery timelines and fulfillment speed.

Cost Optimization: Highlighted trends in shipping costs to manage vendor negotiations or optimize routes.

SLA Monitoring: Showed that ~68% of orders were within SLA—highlighting improvement areas for delayed shipments.

Customer Experience: Enabled proactive resolution of delays and better service reliability.

Data-Driven Planning: Supported strategic logistics decisions based on delivery performance and cost patterns.
